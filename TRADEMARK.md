# The name is not licensed

The **[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE)** covers the code. Section 6 grants no
trademark rights, and this file says plainly what that means here.

## Not licensed

- the names **Radioterapia.AI** and **Workstation Kit**
- the logos and the visual identity in `docs/img/`
- the domain **radioterapia.ai** and anything that suggests it

You may not use them to identify a derived product, or in a way that suggests the
maintainers endorse, support or reviewed your work.

## A fork may keep

- the source, under the Apache licence, with the [NOTICE](NOTICE) alongside it
- the file and function names, the structure, the registry paths
- a **factual** statement of origin: "based on Workstation Kit by Radioterapia.AI"
  is fine. "Workstation Kit Pro" is not.

## Why it is spelled out

This tool closes processes on a workstation that may be in clinical use. Its
promise rests on protection lists, on reversibility, and on a gate that runs on
every change. A fork can change any of those — that is the licence working as
intended. What it cannot do is change them and keep the name, because then the
promise would be read from one project and delivered by another.
